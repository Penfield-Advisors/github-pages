Track record

As President of Virtual Services at Fusion Education Group, a $250M+ private equity-backed platform, {{ site.founder.name }} built Fusion Global Academy from nothing into the largest and fastest-growing school launch in company history, moved 80+ campuses to virtual operations without material enrollment loss, and widened the course catalog by 25%.

Board and senior advisory work includes Senior Advisor and Chief of Staff to the CEO of The Center for Education Reform, where the mandate was turning priorities into plans and driving them through the organization; Board Director at Apple Montessori Schools; and founding Board Director of the Pennsylvania Coalition of Public Charter Schools.
