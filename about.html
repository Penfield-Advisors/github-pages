{{ site.founder.name }}

{{ site.founder.name }} is the Founder and Managing Director of Penfield Advisors LLC, launched in 2024 after more than 25 years leading education organizations through growth, transformation, and ownership change. The firm's work draws directly on that operating experience: strategic planning, online and hybrid learning models, business development, and organizational effectiveness.

Career highlights

  • Delivered 5x revenue and EBITDA growth in two years
  • Sustained 22% CAGR over five years, growing revenue from $7M to $23M
  • Grew enrollment from 900 to 5,000+ learners and retention from 32% to 72%
  • Managed divisions with $25M+ P&L responsibility and teams of 250+
  • Expanded operations across all 50 states and 33+ countries
  • Led ownership transitions, acquisitions, integrations, and platform migrations

Background

As President of Virtual Services at Fusion Education Group, a $250M+ private equity-backed platform, {{ site.founder.name }} launched and scaled Fusion Global Academy, the largest and fastest-growing school launch in company history, and moved 80+ campuses to virtual operations without material enrollment loss.

Earlier roles include Senior Vice President at Nobel Learning Communities and President of Laurel Springs School, serving on the executive team through a public-to-private conversion and multiple private equity transactions, and nearly a decade at K12 Inc. leading school launches, accreditation, and international expansion. The career began on the founding leadership team of a Philadelphia charter school.

Board and advisory service includes Senior Advisor and Chief of Staff to the CEO of The Center for Education Reform, Board Director at Apple Montessori Schools, and founding Board Director of the Pennsylvania Coalition of Public Charter Schools.
